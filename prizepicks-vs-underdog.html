PrizePicks vs Underdog: Which DFS App Is Better for California Players?

Short answer: if you want simple player-pick entries, PrizePicks is the simpler pick. If you want drafts and Best Ball as well as picks, Underdog is the broader app. Both are daily fantasy, not a California sportsbook — and neither referral reward is cash.

How each one works

PrizePicks is built around Player Picks: you pick 2 or more players and choose More or Less on a projected stat (points, rebounds, passing yards, and similar). Get the picks right and the entry pays out at a multiplier shown before you submit it. There is no draft, no opponent lineup to beat, and no salary cap to manage — just your picks against the projections.

Underdog does two different things. Its DFS side includes drafts, Best Ball (draft once, no weekly lineups), and Champions contests, plus its own pick-style game, Higher/Lower, which works like PrizePicks' More/Less: pick players and choose whether they go higher or lower than a projected number. Underdog also runs Underdog Predict, a separate prediction-markets product. The referral bonus entries described below are for fantasy contest entries — not directly for Predict.
