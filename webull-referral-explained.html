Webull Referral Explained: The $100 Deposit, the Fractional Shares, and the 30-Day Clocks

Webull's referral is the highest-bar offer on this site: the friend has to open a real brokerage account and deposit $100 within 30 days. In exchange, the referrer gets fractional shares — usually worth about $3.

Disclosure: I earn a commission if you sign up through my link. This page is about the Webull referral program only. Terms were checked in October 2026 and terms change — check Webull's live terms before you sign up.

How it works, step by step

  1. The friend signs up using the referral link.
  2. The friend opens a standard brokerage account — not a prediction-markets-only account (see the trap below).
  3. The friend deposits $100 or more within 30 days of signing up.
  4. The reward posts within about 10 days of the deposit.
  5. The referrer claims the reward in the Webull app within 30 days of it being issued.

What the referrer actually gets

The referrer gets fractional shares. The typical value is about $3 — about 98% of awarded shares are worth about $3, with a range of $3–$300 depending on which shares are awarded. The exact shares and the exact campaign terms vary by campaign, so treat "$3" as typical, not guaranteed.

Conflicting figures, flagged honestly: some sources describe the Webull referral reward as "up to 20 free stocks." The October 2026 research used for this page reports fractional shares typically worth about $3 (about 98% of the time, range $3–$300). This page uses the ~$3 figure because it matches the per-referral experience most sources describe; check Webull's live referral terms for the current campaign before counting on any specific number of shares.

The shares are shares, not cash on arrival. They have value and can be sold, but the reward is not a cash deposit into your bank account.

The friend's side

The friend's welcome offer, as described in the October 2026 research, is 10 fractional shares ($3–$300 each; about 98% at $3) on a $100+ deposit, plus 1 month of Premium. The deposit is the qualifying action: $100 or more within 30 days of signing up. Joint accounts, IRA accounts, and entity accounts are excluded from the referral program as described in the research.

The trap: prediction-only signups do NOT trigger the referral

This is the detail most people miss. If the friend signs up for Webull's prediction markets only — without opening a standard brokerage account and making the deposit — the referral does not trigger. No reward is earned. The friend must open a standard brokerage account and deposit $100 within 30 days. If someone only wants to try prediction markets, the Webull referral is the wrong link for them.

The two 30-day clocks — do not mix them up

  • Deposit window (30 days): the friend must deposit $100+ within 30 days of signing up. Miss it and the referral does not qualify.
  • Claim window (30 days): once the reward is issued, it must be claimed in the Webull app within 30 days of issue. An unclaimed reward lapses.

The reward posts within about 10 days of the deposit, so the claim clock usually starts roughly 10 days after the deposit lands.

What this page does not claim

The October 2026 research used here did not state a sell or withdraw timeline for Webull rewards, so this page does not guess at one. Do not assume Webull's timing matches Robinhood's 3-day sell / 30-day withdraw windows — check Webull's live terms for when shares can be sold and when cash can be withdrawn.

Who this suits — honestly

This suits someone who already wants a Webull brokerage account and has $100 they are comfortable depositing anyway. It does not suit someone looking for free money with no deposit, or someone who only wants prediction markets — the $100 deposit and the brokerage account are real requirements, and the typical reward is about $3 in shares.
