The refer-a-friend offer, plainly

  • Referrer gets: $10 in bonus entries per friend, plus a 1-in-1,000 shot at $2,000. Some guides put the referrer amount at $10–$25; this page uses the $10 baseline so it does not overstate the typical case.
  • Friend must: deposit $10. Several guides add: and play once.
  • Timing: credit within about 72 hours.
  • Limit: one bonus per friend.
  • Friend's welcome offer is separate: commonly Play $5, Get $50 in bonus entries. That is the friend's welcome offer, not the referrer's bonus, and the two should not be added together as if they were one payout.

What bonus entries are — and are not

Bonus entries are not cash. They are not withdrawable. They expire in 14 days. Their only value is in entries to contests. If you sign up expecting withdrawable money from bonus entries, you will be disappointed — that is exactly the misunderstanding this page is trying to prevent.
