Robinhood Referral Explained: Why Almost Everyone Gets $5 (and the Milestone Math)

Disclosure: I earn a commission if you sign up through my link. This page explains Robinhood's referral offer as checked in October 2026. Terms change — check Robinhood's live terms before you sign up.

Why almost everyone gets $5

The friend's reward is a stock, not a fixed cash amount. In the October 2026 summary, that stock is usually about $5 — roughly 99% of the time — with a stated range of $5 to $200. That is why the headline can sound bigger than what most people actually receive: the $200 end of the range exists, but it is the exception, not the expectation.

Put plainly: expect about $5 in stock value. Anything above that is a bonus, not a plan.

What the friend has to do

  1. Open a brokerage account through the referral link.
  2. Link bank/funding.
  3. Make a qualifying deposit.
  4. Claim within 60 days.

Miss the 60-day claim window and the reward is not something to assume you can recover later — check live terms if you are past it.

When the money actually becomes usable

  • After 3 trading days: the stock becomes sellable.
  • After 30 days: cash becomes withdrawable.

That two-step timing matters. Selling is not the same as withdrawing. If you need cash in hand, the relevant date is the 30-day mark, not the day the stock arrives.

Do the maths on referrals you actually expect, not on the top milestone: getting to 200 funded referrals is a very different job from getting to one.
