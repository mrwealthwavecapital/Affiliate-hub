Underdog Predict in California: What It Is, How Contracts Settle, and What Else You Can Play

What Underdog Predict is

Underdog Predict is a prediction market: you trade contracts on whether an event outcome will happen — a game result, an award, an election outcome, and similar events. Each contract has a price that reflects what the market thinks the chance of that outcome is, and the price moves as that view changes.

Predict runs through CFTC-regulated exchanges, including Underdog's own UDX exchange and partner exchanges. That matters because it is a different legal category from sports betting: prediction markets are regulated as financial-style contracts under federal commodities law, not as state sports wagering. That is why Predict can operate in California while a traditional sportsbook cannot.

How contracts settle

When the event's outcome is determined, contracts settle: the winning side is paid out and the losing side is not. Three things decide whether you get paid, and they are set by each market's rules:

  • The payout value per contract — what a winning contract is worth.
  • The settlement source — who decides the outcome and how disputes are handled. This is the part that decides whether you get paid, so read it on the specific market before you trade it.
  • Timing — settlement happens after the outcome is determined and confirmed under the market's rules. It is not instant at the final whistle or the announcement.

Two markets that look alike can settle differently. Read the settlement rules on the specific market before you trade it, and never trade money you cannot afford to lose.

What else you can play in California

Predict is not the only California-legal option on Underdog. The others are the daily fantasy formats:

  • Drafts — draft a fantasy lineup and compete against other players' lineups.
  • Best Ball — draft once; your best lineup is set automatically each week, with no lineup management during the season.
  • Champions — season-long contests with a championship structure.

These are the formats multiple 2026 guides list as available in California, alongside Predict.

What you cannot play in California

There is no Underdog sportsbook in California. Online sports betting is not legal in California, and Underdog's sportsbook licenses are in other states (North Carolina live, Ohio licensed, Missouri upcoming). Never let anyone sell you a "California Underdog sportsbook" — it does not exist.

Pick'em in California: contested — both sides, honestly

Pick'em — picking whether players will go over or under a stat line — is the one format this page will not give you a clean yes or no on, because the sources disagree:

  • The case that it is not available in CA: two current guides list Classic Pick'em in only 15 states, and California is not among them.
  • The case that it is available in CA: another state table lists California Pick'em as available.
  • The background: a 2025 California Attorney General opinion concluded that pick'em and draft-style DFS contests constitute illegal sports wagering; the Governor's office disagreed, and no enforcement has followed. Operators continue to operate in California.

The safe approach: promote drafts, Best Ball, Champions, and Predict in California, and do not make Pick'em the headline California offer. If you want to know whether Pick'em is available to you specifically, verify it inside the Underdog app before playing — do not rely on this page or any single guide for that question.
