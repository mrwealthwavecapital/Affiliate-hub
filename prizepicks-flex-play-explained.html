PrizePicks Flex Play Explained: What Happens When One Pick Misses?

Short answer: with Flex Play, one missed pick does not always zero your entry — depending on the entry, you can still win a smaller payout. With Power Play, every pick has to hit or you win nothing. Flex pays less when you are perfect, and that is the trade.

Do not quote a specific multiplier from this page as fixed: Flex payouts and multipliers vary by entry size and by the picks in the entry. The multiplier for your exact entry is shown in the app before you submit it — that is the number that counts.

How to choose

  • Pick Flex when you like the entry but one leg feels shaky. You are buying insurance with a lower top payout.
  • Pick Power Play when you want the maximum payout and you accept that one miss ends the entry.
  • Check the payout table in the app before you submit either way. If the Flex payout for your entry size looks too small to be worth it, that is useful information — do not enter it just because Flex exists.
