How to Claim Your Robinhood Referral Stock, Step by Step

Disclosure: I earn a commission if you sign up through my link. This page walks through Robinhood's referral claim steps as checked in October 2026. Terms change — check Robinhood's live terms before you sign up.

A Robinhood referral is not one action. It is a chain of small steps, and the reward depends on every link in the chain holding. This page walks through the steps in order, says where each one happens, and flags the places people most often get stuck.

The steps, in order

  1. Use the referral link to sign up. Robinhood's referral works by link only — there is no promo code to type in. The friend must be new to Robinhood, a US resident, and 18 or older.
  2. Open a brokerage account and get approved. This is a real brokerage account application, not a signup form. Approval is required before anything else can happen.
  3. Link a bank or funding source. The account needs a linked funding source before the deposit step can be completed.
  4. Make a qualifying deposit. The deposit must settle. The exact qualifying amount is set by Robinhood's live terms — check it before you deposit, because it is the term most likely to have changed since this page was checked.
  5. Claim the reward within 60 days. This is the step people miss. The referral stock does not always arrive on its own — look for it in the app's Rewards section and claim it. If you sign up and then forget about the account, the 60-day window can pass and the reward may be gone.
  6. Wait 3 trading days before selling. The stock is typically sellable after 3 trading days. Selling is not the same as withdrawing.
  7. Wait 30 days before withdrawing cash. Cash from the referral stock is typically withdrawable after 30 days. If you need cash in hand, the 30-day mark is the date that matters, not the day the stock arrives.

What the stock is actually worth

The friend's reward is a stock worth $5 to $200, and about 99% of the time it is worth about $5. Treat $5 as the expectation. The stock's value can also move after you receive it — a $5 stock is not $5 in guaranteed cash. If $5 would not be worth opening and funding a brokerage account to you, the referral alone is not a reason to open one.

Where people get stuck

  • The deposit never settles. A deposit that is still pending does not count as a qualifying deposit. Wait for it to settle, then check.
  • The 60-day claim window passes. This is the most ordinary way to lose the bonus. Set a reminder the day you sign up.
  • The stock is there but not sellable yet. The 3-trading-day hold is normal, not an error. Trading days exclude weekends and market holidays, so "3 days" on a calendar can take longer.
  • The cash is there but not withdrawable yet. The 30-day hold is also normal. Selling early does not shorten it.
  • The wrong account type. The referral requires a standard individual brokerage account. Check the live terms if you are opening anything else.

If the reward still has not arrived after the deposit has settled and the claim window is open, the troubleshooting page Why Your Referral Bonus Didn't Arrive walks through the usual causes for all four offers on this site.

What the person who referred you gets

Since September 2026, referrer rewards are milestones, not a flat amount per friend: $50 at 1 funded referral, $150 at 3, $800 at 10, $4,000 at 50, and $10,000 at 200. This page does not claim the milestone amounts stack cumulatively — the October 2026 summary lists the amount at each milestone, and the live terms are the authority on how they stack. There is an annual cap on referrer rewards: the October 2026 summary gives $15,000 per year, while older sources cite $1,500 per year. The two figures conflict, so verify the cap in Robinhood's live terms before relying on it.
