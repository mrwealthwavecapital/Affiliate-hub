Webull Referral: What the $100 Deposit Rule Actually Requires

Webull's referral looks simple — your friend signs up, deposits $100, you both get fractional shares. The part that trips people up is the deposit itself: it has to land in a standard brokerage account, inside 30 days, and a prediction-markets-only signup does not count at all. Here is what the rule actually requires, clock by clock.

The rule, in one paragraph

Your friend must open a standard Webull brokerage account through your referral link and deposit $100 within 30 days of signing up. When that happens, the referrer gets fractional shares per successful referral (the exact number is campaign-dependent) and the friend gets the welcome offer: 10 fractional shares ($3–$300 each, about 98% at $3) on a $100+ deposit, plus 1 month of Premium. The reward posts within about 10 days of the deposit and must be claimed in-app within 30 days of issue — miss the claim window and it is gone.

What counts as the $100 deposit

The deposit is a $100 cash deposit into the standard brokerage account, made within 30 days of signup. That is the requirement as Webull states it in the October 2026 research. What the research does not specify is whether a transfer of securities from another broker counts as the deposit, or whether the $100 must arrive as cash — if you are planning to fund the account by transferring holdings rather than depositing cash, check Webull's live terms before you rely on it. Do not assume a transfer counts.

The trap: prediction-markets-only signups do NOT trigger the referral

This is the most expensive mistake in the Webull program. If your friend signs up for Webull's prediction markets only — without opening a standard brokerage account and depositing — the referral does not trigger, and nobody gets anything. Webull's prediction-markets product is a separate signup path, and it does not count toward the brokerage referral. If your friend only wants prediction markets, the referral is the wrong link for them. The full explainer is on the Webull referral explained page.

Who is excluded

Joint accounts, IRA accounts, and entity accounts are excluded from the referral program. The friend must be a new individual brokerage account holder. The referral link itself lives in the app: Menu → Promotion Center → Referral Bonus → Invite.

The three clocks, in order

There are three separate deadlines, and each one can eat the reward on its own:

  • 30 days to deposit — the friend's $100 must land within 30 days of signup. After that, the referral no longer qualifies.
  • About 10 days to post — the reward posts within roughly 10 days of the deposit. If nothing appears after 10 days, check the Promotion Center before assuming it failed.
  • 30 days to claim — once issued, the reward must be claimed in-app within 30 days of issue. An unclaimed reward expires.

What you actually get — honestly

The typical fractional share is about $3 — roughly 98% of the welcome shares are $3 shares, and the referrer's shares follow the same campaign-dependent pattern. This is not $100 of free stock for a $100 deposit; it is a small number of fractional shares worth a few dollars each, sellable once claimed.
