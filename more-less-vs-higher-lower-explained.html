More/Less vs Higher/Lower: How DFS Pick Games Actually Work

Short answer: they are the same idea under two names. You pick players and predict whether each one goes More or Less — or Higher or Lower — than a projected stat. No draft, no salary cap, no opponent lineup. Your picks are judged against the projections, and the payout multiplier is shown before you submit.

How a pick entry works, step by step

  1. Pick 2 or more players (the minimum and maximum depend on the entry type — check the app).
  2. For each player, choose More (PrizePicks) / Higher (Underdog) or Less / Lower on a projected stat — points, rebounds, passing yards, and similar.
  3. Choose your entry type (for example, PrizePicks Flex Play vs Power Play — see PrizePicks Flex Play Explained).
  4. Check the payout multiplier shown before you submit. That is what the entry pays if it wins.

What it is not

  • Not a draft. There is no salary cap to manage and no lineup to set against an opponent. You are playing your picks against the projections.
  • Not a sportsbook bet. In California, where there is no legal online sportsbook, these are daily fantasy pick games — that is why they are available in CA when a sportsbook is not.
  • Not a sure payout. A multiplier is what an entry pays if it wins. It is not a return on the entry, and entries can lose the whole amount.
