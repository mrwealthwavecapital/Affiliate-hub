How Long Does a Robinhood Referral Bonus Take? The 60-Day Clock Explained

A Robinhood referral bonus is not one payment on one day — it is a chain of clocks. Your friend has 60 days to claim, the stock takes 3 trading days before it can be sold, and the cash takes 30 days before it can be withdrawn. And since September 2026, the referrer is paid on milestones, not per friend. Here is the whole timeline, in order.

First, the milestone change: you are not paid per friend any more

Since September 1, 2026, Robinhood pays referrers on milestones only: $50 at 1 funded referral, $150 at 3, $800 at 10, $4,000 at 50, and $10,000 at 200 funded referrals, with a $15,000 per year cap. There is no per-friend payment any more — one funded referral earns the $50 milestone, and the second earns nothing until the third, which completes the $150 milestone. Older sources citing a $1,500 cap are superseded by this milestone structure. The friend's side is unchanged: a free stock worth $5–$200 (published odds about 99% $5, 0.9% $10, 0.1% $200).

The 60-day clock: your friend must claim in time

The friend must claim within 60 days. That is the first and most-missed deadline: the referral does not complete itself. The friend must be new to Robinhood, approved, with a linked bank account and a settled deposit. If the friend never claims, the referral never counts — and it does not count toward your next milestone either. If your friend signed up and nothing happened, the 60-day claim window is the first thing to check. The step-by-step claim walkthrough is on the Robinhood claim steps page.

The 3-day clock: when the stock can be sold

Once claimed, the free stock is sellable after 3 trading days. Trading days, not calendar days — a Friday claim means the stock is sellable the following Wednesday, not Monday. The stock itself is not cash: it is a share (usually about $5) that sits in the account until you sell it.

The 30-day clock: when the cash can leave

After selling, the cash is withdrawable after 30 days. This is the clock that surprises people most: the money is in the account, the sale has settled, and it still cannot be withdrawn for a month. The full cash-out timeline — deposit to sell to claim — is worked out on the cash-out calculator, and the milestone math is on the Robinhood referral explained page.

The whole timeline, end to end

Put together, a referral looks like this: your friend signs up through your link and links a bank account → they deposit (settled deposit required) → they claim the free stock within 60 days → the stock is sellable after 3 trading days → the cash from selling is withdrawable after 30 days. On the referrer side, the milestone credits when the referral is funded and claimed — $50 at the first, $150 at the third, $800 at the tenth, and so on up to the $15,000 yearly cap. From signup to withdrawable cash, the honest answer is roughly five to six weeks at the fastest — and longer if the friend is slow to claim.
