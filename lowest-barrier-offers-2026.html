The honest point: lowest deposit does not mean fastest cash

Polymarket US and Underdog tie for the lowest cash-in at $10, and both pay in play credit that can never be withdrawn as-is. Robinhood asks for a qualifying deposit with no $100 bar, and its reward — a stock, usually about $5 — is the only one here with a stated path to withdrawable cash (sell after 3 trading days, withdraw cash after 30 days). Webull has the highest bar at $100, but its reward is fractional shares with real value, not entries or credit. So the right question is not "which needs the least money" — it is "which reward, after its hold or expiry, is worth anything I can use?"
