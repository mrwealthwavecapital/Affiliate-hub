The offer, step by step

The $10 is the qualifying step — this is not a signup-only reward. The friend must deposit $10 for either side to get the credit.

  1. The friend signs up through the referral link and opens a Polymarket US account.
  2. The friend deposits $10. Without that deposit, no credit is issued to anyone.
  3. Each side receives $25 in trading credit — the referrer and the friend — credited within about 7 days of all requirements being met.
  4. The credit is used to trade event contracts on Polymarket US.

What the $25 credit can and can't do

  • It is trading credit — not withdrawable cash. The $25 itself cannot be withdrawn as cash.
  • It can be traded with. Use it to buy event contracts; only the trading proceeds, under the live terms, could become withdrawable — that is different from cashing out the credit.
  • Expiry: this page does not assert a single expiry length for the credit, because it was not stated in the research used here — check the live terms before counting on a use window.
  • Cap: up to 50 referrals, which is up to $1,250 in trading credit at the $25 figure.

What you can trade with it

Polymarket US trades event contracts — yes/no positions on real-world outcomes (elections, sports, markets, and similar). A contract typically settles at $1 if the outcome happens and $0 if it does not, and the price you pay reflects how likely the market thinks the outcome is. If you have never traded one, read Prediction Markets for Beginners: How Contracts Actually Settle first, and compare the platforms on Kalshi vs Polymarket vs Robinhood Prediction Markets. Trading involves risk, and a $25 credit can be lost in full on losing trades — only trade money you can afford to lose.

Don't confuse this with the fee-share program

Polymarket has two different programs, and they are easy to mix up. The one on this page is the US refer-a-friend program: $25 trading credit each after a $10 deposit. The other is the international fee-share program, which pays a share of referrals' trading fees — and requires $10,000 in lifetime trading volume before earning anything. That $10,000 catch does not apply to the US refer-a-friend credit. See Polymarket Fee-Share vs Refer-a-Friend and Polymarket Referral: The $10,000 Catch for the full distinction.

Who this suits — honestly

  • It suits someone who already wants to trade event contracts and would deposit $10 anyway. For that person, $25 in trading credit is $25 of trades they did not have to fund.
  • It does not suit someone looking for $25 in withdrawable cash, because that is not what the reward is. It also does not suit someone who will not deposit $10 — without the deposit, there is no credit at all.
