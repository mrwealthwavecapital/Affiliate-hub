Why Your Referral Bonus Didn't Arrive (and How to Fix It)

Your friend signed up through your link and nothing showed up. Most missing bonuses are a missed qualifying step, a clock that has not run out yet, or a reward that went somewhere you did not look. The causes below come from terms checked in October 2026.

Disclosure: I earn a commission if you sign up through my link. Terms were checked in October 2026 and terms change — check each platform's live referral terms and the rewards section of its app before concluding a bonus is lost.

First, the cause that fits every platform

On all four programs, a referral only counts when the friend finishes the whole qualifying step — account approved, bank linked, deposit made — using your link (or username/code where one is required) at signup. A friend who used a different link is usually not attached to your referral at all.

Second, check the timing: every program below has a delay between qualifying and the reward appearing.

Robinhood: the stock is there, or the friend never qualified

  • The friend was not new, or was not approved. The referral counts only for a new customer who is approved, links a bank, and makes a qualifying deposit. An existing account, a rejected application, or a deposit that never settled all mean no referral.
  • You are looking in the wrong place. The friend's free stock (usually $5, range $5–$200) is claimed in the friend's account; the referrer's milestone lands in the referrer's rewards. Check the right account.
  • The 60-day claim window passed. The friend must claim the free stock within 60 days or it lapses — a stock never claimed is gone, not delayed.
  • You expected a per-referral payment. Since September 2026, Robinhood pays referrers on milestones only — $50 at 1, $150 at 3, $800 at 10, $4,000 at 50, $10,000 at 200. The research does not say whether milestones stack, and older sources citing a $1,500/year cap are superseded by the milestone structure. Check the live terms in your own rewards section.
  • The stock arrived but the cash did not. That is normal: the stock is sellable after 3 trading days, but cash is withdrawable only 30 days after the deposit — see the cash-out calculator for exact dates.

Webull: the $100 deposit and the prediction-markets trap

  • The deposit was under $100, or landed after 30 days. A $50 deposit, or a $100 deposit on day 31, does not qualify.
  • The friend opened prediction markets only. A prediction-markets-only signup does not trigger the referral — the friend must open a standard brokerage account and deposit.
  • Joint, IRA, or entity account. Those account types are excluded.
  • The reward posted but was never claimed. Rewards post within about 10 days of the deposit and must be claimed in-app within 30 days of issue — check the promotion center.
  • You are still inside the 10-day posting window. If the deposit landed less than 10 days ago, the reward is not late yet.

The October 2026 research did not state a sell or withdraw timeline for Webull rewards — check Webull's live terms rather than assuming Robinhood's 3-day / 30-day windows.

Underdog: it is probably the 72 hours, or the 14 days

  • It has been less than 72 hours. The $10 in bonus entries is credited within about 72 hours of the friend's $10 deposit (several guides add: and playing once). Less than three days in, the entries are not late.
  • The entries arrived and expired. Bonus entries expire 14 days after they are credited — if you did not use them, they are gone, and they were never cash.
  • You are looking for cash. Bonus entries are not cash and cannot be withdrawn — only winnings from contests entered with them can become cash.
  • One bonus per friend. A friend who signed up twice does not earn a second bonus.
  • California note: no Underdog sportsbook here — drafts, Best Ball, Champions, and Predict only. Pick'em availability is contested; verify in-app. 18+.

Polymarket US: the 7-day window, or the wrong program

  • It has been less than 7 days. The $25 trading credit is added within about 7 days of the friend's $10 deposit — inside that window, it is not late.
  • You are looking for cash. The credit is trading-only and can never be withdrawn directly — it must be traded first, and only proceeds above the credit may be withdrawable. A credit that arrived but cannot be withdrawn is working as designed.
  • You are thinking of the fee-share program. Fee-share pays a share of referrals' trading fees only after the referrer has $10,000 of lifetime trading volume — nothing to do with the $25 refer-a-friend credit, which has no volume threshold.
  • You hit the cap. The US program pays up to 50 referrals ($1,250 total); one source reports a $350 cap instead. Referrals past the cap earn nothing further.
  • The credit expired. Its expiry length differs across sources and is not verified — check the live campaign terms.

When it is actually gone

Some bonuses are not delayed — they are lost. An unclaimed Robinhood stock after 60 days, an unclaimed Webull reward after 30 days from issue, and Underdog entries unused after 14 days are all gone under the terms as written, and no support ticket restores an expired reward. Next time: claim it the day it lands, and use entries before they expire.

If you have checked everything above and the reward is still nowhere in the app, the remaining step is the platform's in-app support, with the friend's signup and deposit dates in hand. This page cannot contact support for you.
