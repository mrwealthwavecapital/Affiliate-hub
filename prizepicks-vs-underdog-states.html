PrizePicks is now two products in one app. Player Picks is a daily fantasy game (pick 2–6 players, More or Less on a stat). Team Picks and Culture Picks are prediction-market event contracts sold through PrizePicks Predict, a CFTC-registered futures commission merchant, and executed on Kalshi's exchange. Underdog, by state: fantasy contests vs prediction markets

Underdog also splits by product. Its fantasy contests (Drafts, Best Ball, Pick'em, Champions) are state-by-state DFS. Underdog Predict is a separate CFTC-regulated prediction market inside the same app, running on Underdog's own exchange since July 18, 2026 — and it is available in states where fantasy is not. The list below follows SportsHandle, last verified November 11, 2026.

The September 2026 change matters: on September 9–10, 2026, Underdog stopped offering fantasy contests, including Pick'em, Drafts and Best Ball, in Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Mississippi, New Jersey, Ohio and Pennsylvania. Entries made before then settle normally. Prediction markets continue in most of those states. Some older guides still list those seven states as full fantasy states — they are out of date. Michigan is the murkiest of the seven: fantasy definitely ended there; check the app for whether Predict is live for you.

Within the fantasy states, the pick game itself varies: house Pick'em (against the house) is not offered everywhere — California and New York, for example, get Drafts and the peer-to-peer Champions game but not house Pick'em, and Arizona and Alabama get Champions but not house Pick'em. New York gets Drafts but no Pick'em at all. Minimum age is 18 in most states, 19 in Alabama and Nebraska, and 21 in Arizona, Massachusetts and Virginia.

Which app if your state limits both?

  • You only get Culture Picks / Predict where you are (Ohio, Pennsylvania, Michigan, Connecticut, Maryland): neither app gives you the DFS pick game. The choice is between PrizePicks' Culture Picks and Underdog Predict — both are prediction-market contracts, not fantasy. Compare them on the markets you actually want, not on the referral.
  • You get Player Picks but not house Pick'em (New York, California, Arizona): PrizePicks gives you the straightforward More/Less game; Underdog gives you Drafts and Champions instead. See PrizePicks vs Underdog for the game-by-game comparison.
  • You get everything (Texas, Florida, Georgia, California for PrizePicks): pick on the game format you prefer, not the state list. See Best DFS Apps in California in 2026 and PrizePicks in California.
  • You get nothing real-money (Nevada; Washington for PrizePicks): free-to-play games are the only option on these apps. Do not use a VPN to get around a state block — both apps check location and can freeze winnings.
