Underdog Refer-a-Friend: How the $10 Bonus Entries Actually Work (and Expire)

The offer, step by step

  1. The friend signs up with the referrer's username or referral code (the link lives in the Underdog app, in the Refer-a-Friend section — Share Link / Copy Link), verifies their account, and deposits at least $10. Several guides add that the friend must also play or enter a contest once.
  2. The referrer receives $10 in bonus entries per referred friend. The credit lands within about 72 hours of the friend qualifying.
  3. That is the whole reward. There is no cash component.

What the $10 actually is

  • Bonus entries for fantasy contest entries — not cash, not withdrawable. They must be played through in contests.
  • They expire in 14 days if unused. An unused bonus is a lost bonus.
  • One bonus per friend. Referring the same person twice does not pay twice.
  • The jackpot: there is a 1-in-1,000 chance the bonus is $2,000 instead of $10. That is a lottery ticket on top of the referral, not the expected reward — expect $10 in entries.

On the exact reward amount: $10 is the consistent baseline across the October 2026 sources. Some guides report $10–$25 in "Program Credit" depending on the campaign. This page uses $10 and flags the conflict — check the live offer in the app before quoting a higher figure.

What the friend gets (separate offer)

The friend's welcome offer is separate from the referral reward. It is commonly "Play $5, Get $50" in bonus entries (deposit $10, enter $5); some partner pages show a 50% first-deposit match up to $1,000. Welcome offers rotate — never quote one without checking the live offer in the app.

There is no separate public revshare "affiliate program" for ordinary users. What exists for an individual account holder is this in-app refer-a-friend program. Media and publisher promo codes shown on review sites are a different, partnership-based deal.

California: what this offer covers — and what it does not

Underdog does not operate a traditional sportsbook in California — online sports betting is not legal in CA, and Underdog's sportsbook licenses are elsewhere. Never treat this referral as a sportsbook offer in California.

What is live in California: Underdog DFS — drafts, Best Ball, and Champions contests — and Underdog Predict (prediction markets via CFTC-regulated exchanges, including its own UDX exchange and partners). This referral's bonus entries are for fantasy contest entries.

Pick'em in California is contested. Two current guides list Classic Pick'em in only 15 states that do not include CA, while another state table lists CA Pick'em as available. A 2025 California Attorney General opinion concluded pick'em and draft-style DFS contests constitute illegal sports wagering; the Governor's office disagreed and no enforcement has followed. Do not make Pick'em the headline California offer, and verify Pick'em availability inside the app before claiming it.

Age: 18+ in most states including California. Must be present in a state where Underdog Fantasy operates. Terms apply.

Who this suits — honestly

This suits someone who already plays Underdog fantasy contests and has friends who would deposit $10 and play anyway. It does not suit someone looking for $10 in cash, because that is not what the reward is: $10 in entries that expire in 14 days is worth $10 of contest entries and $0 of withdrawable money.
