Polymarket Referral: The $10,000 Catch Nobody Mentions

If a guide promises you a share of your referrals' trading fees from a Polymarket US refer-a-friend signup, it is mixing up two different programs. One of them pays $25 in trading credit after a $10 deposit. The other needs $10,000 in lifetime trading volume before you earn a cent. Here is how to tell them apart — because the link you share decides which one you get.

Program 1 — US refer-a-friend: $25 credit each, no volume threshold

The US refer-a-friend offer is simple: the friend signs up with your link and deposits $10. Both the referrer and the friend receive $25 in trading credit each. The credit is credited within 7 days of all requirements being met. The referrer cap is 50 referrals, which works out to $1,250 in credit at $25 per referral. No $10,000 trading-volume threshold is part of this offer.

What the credit is not: it is not $25 in cash. It is trading credit, it is not directly withdrawable, and only proceeds from trading it may be withdrawable under Polymarket's live terms. On the credit's own use window or expiry, secondary sources differ, so this page does not assert one — check Polymarket's live incentive terms before relying on the credit for a specific trade.

Program 2 — International fee-share: the $10,000 catch

The separate, international fee-share program pays the referrer 10% of direct referrals' net trading fees and 5% of indirect referrals' fees, paid daily in pUSD, for the first 30 days after a referral signs up or until the referral reaches Platinum — whichever comes first (official docs). And the catch: the referrer needs $10,000 in lifetime trading volume before earning anything from this program. Until you have traded $10,000 worth of volume yourself, your fee-share earnings are zero — no matter how many people you referred or how much they traded.

Some 2026 reports describe a boosted 30%/10% fee-share rate for 180 days. The official docs are treated as canonical here and the conflict is flagged — verify live terms. Either way, the volume threshold is the part that decides whether you earn anything at all.

How the mix-up happens

Guides write about "Polymarket referrals" without saying which program they mean, then quote the fee-share percentages next to the refer-a-friend signup steps. The result is a reader who signs up US friends, expects a share of their fees, and gets trading credit instead — or a reader who chases fee-share earnings without knowing about the $10,000 volume wall in front of them. If a guide promises fee-share earnings from a US refer-a-friend signup, it is mixing up two programs.

Which one suits you — honestly

The US refer-a-friend offer suits someone who already wants to trade on prediction markets and would deposit $10 anyway. It does not suit someone looking for $25 in withdrawable cash, because that is not what the credit is. The fee-share program suits someone who already trades at volume — $10,000 lifetime — and refers other active traders. It does not suit a new account with no trading history, because the threshold pays out nothing until it is crossed, and the 30-day window runs from each referral's signup, not from when you cross the threshold.
