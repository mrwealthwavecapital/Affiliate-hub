Underdog Bonus Entries Expiring? What the 14-Day Clock Actually Means

Underdog's refer-a-friend offer gives you $10 in bonus entries per friend — plus a 1-in-1,000 shot at $2,000. The part people miss is the clock: the entries expire 14 days after they are credited. Here is what the 14 days actually count, what happens when they run out, and the mistake that costs people the whole bonus.

The offer, in one paragraph

Underdog's refer-a-friend offer gives the referrer $10 in bonus entries per friend (plus a 1-in-1,000 shot at $2,000). The friend must make a $10 deposit; the entries are credited within about 72 hours of that deposit. The entries expire 14 days after they are credited. They are never cash: they must be played through in Underdog contests, and they cannot be withdrawn.

What the 14 days actually count

The clock starts when the entries are credited, not when your friend signs up and not when they deposit. The credit itself takes up to about 72 hours after the friend's $10 deposit, so the full timeline from deposit to expiry is roughly 17 days — three days of waiting for the credit, then 14 days to use it. If you check your account the day your friend deposits and see nothing, that is normal, not a missing bonus: the entries have not been credited yet.

What happens if the clock runs out

Expired bonus entries are gone. They do not convert to cash, they do not roll over, and there is no grace period in the terms as checked in October 2026. The practical rule is simple: when entries land, use them within the 14 days or lose them. Set the reminder for the day they are credited, not the day your friend signs up.

The mistake that costs people the bonus

The most common mistake is treating bonus entries like cash — waiting for a "good" contest, or assuming they will still be there next month. They will not be. The second mistake is the opposite: playing them on a contest you would never otherwise enter, just to use them before they expire. Bonus entries are worth $10 of entries, not $10 of money; spending them badly to beat the clock is still spending them badly. Use them on a contest you would play anyway, inside the 14 days.

California note

Underdog's California-legal options are daily fantasy formats (Drafts, Best Ball, Champions) and Underdog Predict, the prediction-market product run through CFTC-regulated exchanges. There is no Underdog sportsbook in California. Pick'em in California is contested — sources disagree — so do not make it the headline California offer.
