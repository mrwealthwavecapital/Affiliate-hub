Do you fit? The one-question check

Did you access the CVS Digital Properties — cvs.com, cvshealth.com, or the CVS digital application — in the United States prior to July 27, 2026? If yes, the official site says you are a Class Member and entitled to benefits under the settlement. No purchase is required. The case is Brewer v. CVS Pharmacy, Inc. and Criteo Corp., over alleged disclosure of user data to Criteo and other technology providers through technology embedded on the CVS sites and app. CVS and Criteo deny wrongdoing; the court has not decided who is right.

One claim per household. Amounts are "up to" figures and may shrink pro rata depending on how many valid claims are filed.

What you get, and when

  • Up to $5.00 with no documentation.
  • Up to $10.00 with proof of membership in the settlement class, as the claim form defines it.
  • Payment timing: not soon. The final approval hearing is December 1, 2026, and benefits are distributed only if the court approves the settlement and after any appeals are resolved. Treat this as slow found money, not cash this week.
  • Cost to file: $0. No account needed.

How to file (the official site, and only the official site)

  1. Type CVSDigitalPrivacySettlement.com directly into your browser. Do not click a link in an email about this settlement.
  2. Submit the Claim Form online — the site says that is the fastest way. You can also download the form and mail it to the Settlement Administrator, or email the administrator for a paper copy.
  3. Choose the $5 tier or the $10 tier and supply your own contact and payment details. The claim is a sworn statement: file only if you truly fit, and file it yourself.

Doing nothing means you get no share of the settlement benefits and give up your rights to sue the defendants over these claims. The opt-out and objection deadlines (November 2, 2026) have already passed.
