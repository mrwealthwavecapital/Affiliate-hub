The varies-by-state warning — read this before quoting $25

There is no single published fixed referral figure that applies everywhere. The $25 amount is the commonly reported figure, but PrizePicks' referral reward varies by location/state. A reader in one state may see a different amount from a reader in another. Do not treat $25 as a fixed promise for every state, and do not quote a welcome offer (for example $50 or $150 figures seen on partner pages) as a promise — the welcome offer also varies by state and offer, and this page does not assert one.

What the $25 actually is

  • Bonus Balance / bonus funds — NOT withdrawable cash. The bonus itself cannot be withdrawn.
  • Winnings may become withdrawable. Winnings from eligible Player Picks entered with Bonus Balance may become withdrawable after settlement, under live terms. That is different from withdrawing the bonus itself.
  • Cap: up to 10 referrals in 30 days. At the commonly reported $25 figure, that is up to $250 in Bonus Balance in a 30-day window. Referrals beyond the cap in that window do not earn the bonus.
  • Expiry: this page does not assert a single expiry length for Bonus Balance, because it varies by offer — check live terms in the app before assuming a deadline.
  • One bonus per friend. Referring the same person twice does not pay twice.

What the friend gets (separate offer)

The friend's welcome offer is separate from the referral reward and varies by state and by the offer live at signup. Check live terms in the app before signing up — this page does not quote a welcome figure, because quoting one number would mislead readers in states where a different offer applies.
