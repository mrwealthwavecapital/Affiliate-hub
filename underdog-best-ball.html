Underdog Best Ball Explained for Californians

Best Ball is the lowest-effort way to play season-long fantasy on Underdog: you draft a roster once before the season, and then you do nothing. Each week, your highest-scoring players count automatically. There are no lineups to set, no waivers to chase, and no trades to make.

How Best Ball works

A Best Ball contest is a season-long draft. Before the season starts, you draft a full roster — quarterbacks, running backs, wide receivers, tight ends — in a snake draft against the other entries in your contest. Once the draft is done, your work is finished.

During the season, the format does the managing for you. Each week, the best scores from your roster at each position are counted automatically toward your weekly total. If one of your running backs has a huge week while another is injured, the huge week is the one that counts. You never bench the wrong player, because you never set a lineup at all.

That is the whole appeal, and the whole difference from regular fantasy: the draft is the game. Everything after it is automatic.

How it differs from regular Underdog drafts

Underdog's regular DFS drafts are single-slate contests: you draft a team for one day or one week, and the contest is over when that slate ends. Best Ball stretches the same drafting idea across an entire season. Your roster is locked after the draft, and your score accumulates week by week.

There is also no in-season management of any kind — no waiver wire, no trades, no start/sit decisions. If you enjoy researching and drafting but do not want a weekly chore, Best Ball is built for exactly that. If you enjoy tinkering with lineups every week, a regular draft contest will suit you better.

Is Best Ball legal in California?

Yes, as far as the October 2026 research used for this site can establish: Underdog DFS — drafts, Best Ball, and Champions contests — is live in California. Online sports betting is not legal in California, and Underdog does not operate a sportsbook here, so Best Ball is a fantasy contest, not a sportsbook bet.

Two cautions. First, Pick'em availability in California is contested across sources — two guides list it in only 15 states excluding California, another table lists California as available, and a 2025 California Attorney General opinion called pick'em illegal wagering while the Governor's office disagreed, with no enforcement following. This page makes no claim about Pick'em; verify it inside the app. Second, you must be 18 or older to play in California. If gambling stops being fun, call 1-800-GAMBLER.

What it costs, and what you can win

Entry fees and prize structures vary by contest — Best Ball runs in many contest sizes, from small leagues to large tournaments — so this page does not quote a single entry fee or prize pool. Check the contest lobby inside the Underdog app before entering: the fee, the number of entries, and the payout structure are all shown there for each contest.

What this page can say honestly: Best Ball is entertainment with an entry fee, not an income strategy. The draft is fun, the season is a slow sweat, and most entries do not win. Do not enter with money you need.

How referral bonus entries fit in

Underdog's refer-a-friend program pays the referrer $10 in bonus entries per friend (some guides report $10–$25 by campaign; $10 is the consistent baseline), credited within about 72 hours of the friend's $10 deposit. Bonus entries are used to enter contests — including Best Ball contests — but they are not cash, they cannot be withdrawn, and they expire 14 days after they are credited.

That 14-day clock matters for Best Ball. A season-long contest drafts before the season starts, so bonus entries that land mid-season cannot buy you into a contest whose draft has already happened. If you are sitting on bonus entries, use them on a contest that is actually open — check the lobby — before the 14 days run out. Unused entries are gone after that.

Three honest tips for a first Best Ball draft

  • Draft for the whole season, not week one. Your roster has to survive bye weeks and injuries with nobody to pick up. Depth at every position matters more than in a single-week draft, because there is no waiver wire to fix a thin spot.
  • Do not chase one week's matchup. A player with a great week-one matchup but a bad schedule after that is worth less in Best Ball than in a daily draft — your score is the sum of a whole season of best weeks.
  • Enter once, then stop checking every day. The format is designed to be set-and-forget. Checking daily adds nothing, and it is the fastest way to turn a low-effort contest into a high-effort habit.

The honest summary

Best Ball is Underdog's simplest season-long game: draft once, your best players count automatically, no weekly work. It is live in California as a DFS contest, it is 18+, and it is entertainment — not a way to make money. If you play, enter a contest whose fee you are comfortable losing, and if you are using referral bonus entries, spend them before the 14-day expiry.
