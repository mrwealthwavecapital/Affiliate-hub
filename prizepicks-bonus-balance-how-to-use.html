PrizePicks Bonus Balance: What It Can and Can't Do

Short answer: Bonus Balance is play credit, not money. You can use it to enter Player Picks lineups, and winnings from eligible entries may become withdrawable after settlement — but the Bonus Balance itself can never be withdrawn as cash.

What Bonus Balance is

Bonus Balance is the non-withdrawable credit PrizePicks issues as a bonus — including the referral reward, which is commonly reported as $25 in Bonus Balance per friend who signs up and makes a qualifying deposit. The amount varies by location and state, and there is no single published fixed figure — check live terms. The referral reward can be earned up to 10 times in 30 days (up to $250 at the commonly reported $25 figure).

What it can do

  • Enter Player Picks lineups — including Flex Play entries, under live terms. See PrizePicks Flex Play Explained.
  • Turn into withdrawable winnings — if an eligible Player Picks lineup entered with Bonus Balance wins, the winnings may be credited to your withdrawable balance after settlement, under live terms. That is the only path from Bonus Balance to cash.

What it can't do

  • It cannot be withdrawn directly. Bonus Balance has no cash value on its own. Cashing it out without playing it is not an option.
  • It is not a promise of $25 in your bank account. A $25 referral reward is $25 of entries. Its cash value is $0 unless the entries you play with it win.
  • It is not the same as a deposit. Do not treat it as money you deposited — it does not behave like deposited funds.

Where to find it

Bonus Balance lives in the Rewards tab of the PrizePicks app — the same place your personal referral code is found. If a referral reward is missing, check there first, then check the terms in the Rewards section: the reward depends on the offer active in your state and on the friend having completed a qualifying deposit.

Expiry — check live terms

This page does not assert a single expiry length for Bonus Balance, because it varies by offer. Check the live terms in the app for the expiry on your specific balance, and use it before it expires — an expired bonus is a lost bonus.
