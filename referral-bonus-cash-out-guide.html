How to Cash Out a Referral Bonus

Answer first: a bonus is not cash

Free stock can be sold but not withdrawn straight away. Trading credit can never be withdrawn at all — only profits from trading it can. Bonus entries must be played through before anything becomes withdrawable, and they expire. If you understand that before signing up, you are far less likely to lose the bonus by selling, withdrawing, or waiting too early or too late.

Robinhood: sell, wait 30 days, withdraw

  1. Claim within 60 days.
  2. Wait 3 trading days, then sell.
  3. Wait until 30 days after claiming, then withdraw by ACH to your linked bank. Settlement is normally 1 business day after a sale.

Selling is not withdrawing. The date that matters for cash in hand is the 30-day mark.

Webull: sell, wait, verify

Sources checked in October 2026 point to a similar sell-then-wait pattern, about 30 days to cash out, but Webull's current US offer and terms varied between sources. Do not rely on a share count or timeline from this page alone — check Webull's own Promotion Center and terms on the day you act. If it cannot be verified, treat it as unverified, not as a promise.

Polymarket: the credit is not cash

The bonus is promotional trading credit, not withdrawable cash. You can trade with it; only profits from those trades can be withdrawn, subject to Polymarket's withdrawal rules. The referral code must be entered at registration — it cannot be added later, per the sources checked.

Underdog: play it through, then withdraw

The bonus arrives as bonus entries, not cash. There is a 1x playthrough requirement, and bonus funds expire 14 days after issuance. Delivery varies by account: some accounts receive $50 at once, others receive 5 x $10 daily installments. Present both as variants, not as a single guaranteed delivery.

Mistakes that cost people the bonus

  • Selling or withdrawing too early, before the hold ends.
  • Expecting Polymarket credit to be cash.
  • Letting Underdog bonus entries expire after 14 days.
  • Entering a Polymarket code after registration, when it is too late.
  • Missing a claim window, such as Robinhood's 60 days.

Which bonus is fastest to cash?

Compare the timelines side by side, above, rather than ranking by invented speed. Robinhood and Webull both involve a wait of about a month after claiming. Polymarket never turns credit itself into cash. Underdog turns into cash only after playthrough and before expiry. Your own timing — when you claim, sell, play, and withdraw — decides the actual date.
