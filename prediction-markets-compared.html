The Kalshi rule nobody reads

Kalshi's referral terms prohibit "using the program for a commercial purpose", along with referring yourself, using multiple or false accounts, manufacturing qualifying transactions, and selling or transferring referral benefits. Kalshi may reclaim invalid credit or suspend accounts. That is why there is no Kalshi referral link on this page or anywhere on this site: a public, commercial content campaign promoting a plain refer-a-friend Kalshi link risks bonus denial or account action under that clause. A publisher or media affiliate deal is a separate arrangement with different terms — this site does not have one, so it does not promote a Kalshi link.

There is a second, separate reason to be careful with Kalshi referrals: a pending class action alleges Kalshi's refer-a-friend texts to Washington residents without consent violate Washington's commercial electronic mail law. Do not do cold SMS outreach with referral links, ever.

Do not confuse Polymarket's two programs

Polymarket runs two different referral programs and guides constantly mix them up:

  • US refer-a-friend: $25 trading credit each after a $10 deposit, up to 50 referrals. No $10,000 volume threshold is part of this offer.
  • International fee-share: a share of referred users' trading fees, which needs $10,000 in lifetime trading volume first. That is a different program with different rules.

If a guide promises fee-share earnings from a US refer-a-friend signup, it is mixing up two programs. Use the $25-credit description for the US link below.

Which one is actually worth your first $25?

None of these turns a referral into $25 of withdrawable cash. That is the honest headline. What differs is the path:

  • Kalshi has the tightest clock: the friend must trade $25 within 7 days, and the credit expires 7 days after issue. It suits someone already planning to trade there. It does not suit a referral-chaser, and its terms actively discourage commercial promotion.
  • Polymarket US has the lowest bar to qualify ($10 deposit, no trading-volume threshold) and the highest stated cap (50 referrals, $1,250 in credit). The credit is trading-only, so its value depends entirely on whether you trade well with it.
  • Robinhood Prediction Markets cannot be ranked here on the same terms, because the October 2026 research did not include its fees or referral terms. If prediction markets are your interest and you already use Robinhood's brokerage, check its live terms directly rather than taking a comparison page's word for it.
