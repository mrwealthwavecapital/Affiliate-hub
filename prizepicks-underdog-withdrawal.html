How to Withdraw Winnings from PrizePicks and Underdog: Methods, Minimums, and Timing 2026

Short answer: both apps let you withdraw from $10, charge no withdrawal fee, and pay fastest to an e-wallet — PrizePicks to PayPal, Venmo or Apple Pay, usually under 12 hours; Underdog to PayPal or Trustly, usually 24–72 hours. Bank transfers take 1–5 business days on both. The two rules that catch people out: PrizePicks allows only one successful withdrawal every 24 hours and puts a 72-hour hold on money deposited by bank transfer, and on both apps bonus credit itself can never be withdrawn — only winnings from playing with it.

PrizePicks: how to withdraw, step by step

  1. Open the app menu and choose Request Withdrawal.
  2. Enter the amount (minimum $10) and select an approved withdrawal method.
  3. PrizePicks emails you a one-time passcode to confirm; you then get a confirmation once the withdrawal goes through.

Which method you can use depends on how you deposited. A verified personal bank account is the default withdrawal option and can be added without a prior deposit. PayPal, Venmo, Apple Pay and debit cards can receive withdrawals only if you deposited with them first. Credit cards and Discover cards are deposit-only — they never receive withdrawals.

The holds, plainly: money deposited by instant bank transfer waits 72 hours before it can leave the account. Your winnings never wait — payouts are freed from every hold, including bonus playthrough rules. A Deposit Bonus can separately require a 1x playthrough on the deposited amount before that deposit (not your winnings) can be withdrawn. First withdrawals can take 6–12 hours while a security review runs; after that, e-wallet withdrawals are often completed within minutes to hours. The terms allow up to 5 business days, or up to 10 when tax paperwork is required, and PrizePicks advises contacting support if a withdrawal has not arrived within 5 business days. You will need identity verification (personal details, possibly government ID and a selfie) before funds can be released.

Underdog: how to withdraw, step by step

  1. In the app, go to your wallet / account and choose Withdraw.
  2. Pick a method — PayPal, Trustly (online banking), Paysafe, Interchecks (direct deposit, e-check or physical check), or an eligible debit card — and enter the amount (minimum $10, or your whole balance if it is lower).
  3. Confirm with the one-time code sent by text (in the app) or email (on the web). Cancelling and resubmitting restarts the clock.

Which method you can use depends on how you deposited. PayPal and debit cards can usually receive withdrawals only if you deposited with them first; Trustly can be eligible for a withdrawal without a prior Trustly deposit. Venmo cannot fund Underdog predictions, but it can receive a withdrawal if you once deposited with it. Underdog does not offer Venmo as a deposit method for fantasy.

Timing: payment processors typically deliver in 24–72 hours, with PayPal and Trustly fastest at 24–48 hours and electronic check slowest at 3–5 business days. Underdog's Fantasy Terms say it typically endeavours to process requests within 10 business days and may run verification, compliance or anti-fraud checks first — so the terms allow longer than the usual delivery time. Underdog Predict winnings settle into your Underdog wallet first (most single-game markets settle within hours, payouts typically within 24 hours of settlement), and the withdrawal then follows the same wallet rules above. First-time withdrawals may require identity verification.

Why a withdrawal gets stuck

  • You are trying to withdraw bonus credit. On both apps the bonus itself is never cash. PrizePicks Bonus Balance and Underdog bonus entries have to be played; only the winnings from those entries become withdrawable. Underdog bonus entries expire in 14 days if unused.
  • The 72-hour bank hold (PrizePicks). A deposit made by bank transfer cannot be withdrawn for 72 hours. Winnings from that deposit are not held — if your withdrawal is being refused, check whether the amount you are requesting is deposit money rather than winnings.
  • Playthrough on a Deposit Bonus (PrizePicks). Deposits that received a Deposit Bonus carry a 1x playthrough before the deposit itself can leave.
  • One-per-day limit (PrizePicks). Only one successful withdrawal every 24 hours. A second request in the same window will fail.
  • Method mismatch. On both apps, a withdrawal usually has to go back to a method you deposited with (Trustly on Underdog is the main exception). A brand-new PayPal account that never funded the app will not appear as a withdrawal destination.
  • Verification or tax review. Both apps can hold a withdrawal during identity verification or a fraud review. PrizePicks' terms allow up to 10 business days when tax paperwork is needed; Underdog's terms allow up to 10 business days for processing with checks.
  • You cancelled and resubmitted (Underdog). Cancelling a withdrawal and submitting a new one restarts the processing clock.

Which app pays out faster?

On the fastest method, PrizePicks is quicker — PayPal, Venmo and Apple Pay withdrawals are typically under 12 hours, sometimes within minutes once your account is established. Underdog's fastest routes, PayPal and Trustly, typically take 24–48 hours. On bank transfers both take 1–5 business days. Neither charges a withdrawal fee, and both set the minimum at $10.
