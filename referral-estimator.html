1. Which platform's referral program?

Count only friends who would finish the whole qualifying step (open the account, link a bank, and deposit). A signup that never deposits earns nothing on every platform here.

How each estimate is built

  • Robinhood: since September 2026 the referrer earns milestones only — $50 at 1 funded referral, $150 at 3, $800 at 10, $4,000 at 50, $10,000 at 200 — with a $15,000/year cap. The research does not say whether milestones add up, so this estimator reports only the highest milestone you reach, never a sum. Older sources cite a $1,500/year cap; the milestone structure is the newer figure. The friend usually gets a $5 stock (range $5–$200).
  • Webull: the referrer gets fractional shares per successful referral, typically worth about $3 (about 98% of awarded shares are about $3; range $3–$300). Some sources describe "up to 20 free stocks" — flagged as a conflict, not used as the estimate. The friend must deposit $100+ within 30 days and open a standard brokerage account; prediction-only signups do not trigger it.
  • Underdog: $10 in bonus entries per referred friend (some guides report $10–$25 by campaign; $10 is the consistent baseline), plus a 1-in-1,000 shot at a $2,000 jackpot per referral. Bonus entries are never cash and expire in 14 days.
  • Polymarket US: $25 in trading credit per referral, for the referrer and the friend, after the friend deposits $10 — up to 50 referrals ($1,250 total). One source reports a $350 cap instead; flagged as a conflict. The credit is trading-only and can never be withdrawn as cash directly.
