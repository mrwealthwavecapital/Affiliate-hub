Polymarket Fee-Share vs Refer-a-Friend: Two Different Programs, and the $10,000 Catch

Polymarket has two referral programs, and guides constantly mix them up. One pays a share of trading fees — but only after you have traded $10,000 yourself. The other pays a $25 trading credit per friend. They are not the same offer, and which one applies to you depends on where you are and what you have already done.

The fee-share program — and the $10,000 catch

The fee-share program pays the referrer 10% of direct referrals' net trading fees and 5% of indirect referrals' net fees, paid daily in pUSD. The earning window is the first 30 days after the referral signs up, or until the referral reaches Platinum status — whichever comes first, according to Polymarket's official docs.

But before any of that pays anything, the referrer must have $10,000 in lifetime trading volume on Polymarket. Until that threshold is met, the fee-share program earns the referrer nothing at all — no matter how many people sign up through the link. The signup must also occur within 30 days of the click.

The US refer-a-friend link — the $25 credit

The US refer-a-friend program is the simpler one, and it is the one the link on this page belongs to:

  • The friend signs up with the referral and deposits $10.
  • Both the referrer and the friend receive $25 in trading credit each.
  • The credit is credited within about 7 days of all requirements being met — that is the crediting window, not an expiry claim.
  • The referrer cap is 50 referrals, which works out to $1,250 in credit at $25 per referral.

The credit is trading credit, not cash. It is not directly withdrawable; you trade with it, and only trading results — under the live terms — could become withdrawable. That is different from cashing out the credit itself.

How to tell which program a guide is describing

  • If it talks about percentages of trading fees, pUSD, and a $10,000 volume threshold, it is the fee-share program.
  • If it talks about a $25 credit, a $10 deposit, and a 50-referral cap, it is the US refer-a-friend program.
  • If it promises fee-share earnings from a US refer-a-friend signup, it is mixing the two programs up.

There is also a separate formal affiliate arrangement via Dub ($10 per first deposit + $0.01 per click + 20% Perps revenue share) that requires an application and a channel. That is a third thing again, and it is not the link on this page.

Which one suits you — honestly

The US refer-a-friend link suits someone who already wants to trade on Polymarket and would deposit $10 anyway. The fee-share program suits someone who already trades serious volume — $10,000 in lifetime volume before it pays anything — and has an audience of traders to refer. For most people, the $25 credit is the realistic one, and even it is trading credit, not cash.
