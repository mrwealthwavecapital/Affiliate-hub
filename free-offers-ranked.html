The honest takeaway

None of these is instant cash. If you need money you can withdraw, Robinhood is the only offer on this page with a stated withdrawable-cash timeline. Webull's shares have value but need a $100 deposit first and have no stated withdraw timeline in the research used here. Polymarket's $25 is trading credit, not cash. Underdog's $10 is contest entries that expire in 14 days and can never be withdrawn. Pick the offer for what it actually is, not for its headline number.
