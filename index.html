Ranked by how fast you can actually cash out

This ranking is about cash you can withdraw — not headline dollar amounts. A $25 credit that can never be withdrawn as cash ranks below a $5 stock that eventually can be.

  1. 1. Robinhood — closest to cash. The friend's stock is usually about $5, sellable after 3 trading days, with cash withdrawable after 30 days. That 30-day wait is the clearest cash-out path on this page.
  2. 2. Webull — shares with value, but a higher bar. Fractional shares have real value, but the friend must deposit $100+ within 30 days, versus Robinhood's qualifying-deposit route. The October 2026 summary used here did not state a sell/withdraw timeline for Webull, so do not assume it matches Robinhood's — check live terms.
  3. 3. Polymarket US — trading value only. The $25 credit can be traded with, but the credit itself is not withdrawable cash. Only trading results, under the live terms, could become withdrawable — that is a different thing from cashing out the credit.
  4. 4. Underdog — bonus entries only. Bonus entries are not cash and cannot be withdrawn. They expire in 14 days. Their value is only in entries, not in money out.

The honest takeaway: none of these is instant cash. If you need money you can withdraw, Robinhood is the only one on this page with a stated withdrawable-cash timeline in the October 2026 summary. The others are credits, shares, or entries first.
