The honest difference

Robinhood is the lower bar and the clearer cash-out path. The friend's reward is usually about $5, the claim window is 60 days, and the path from stock to withdrawable cash is spelled out: sell after 3 trading days, withdraw after 30 days.

Webull asks for more up front. A $100+ deposit within 30 days is a real commitment for a beginner, and the typical referrer reward in the summary is about $3 in fractional shares. That does not make Webull a bad platform — it makes it a worse choice if your only goal is the smallest possible step to a first reward.

The Webull trap to avoid: signing up for prediction markets only. That route does not trigger this referral. If you use the Webull link intending to qualify, open the standard brokerage account and meet the deposit rule, and claim in-app within 30 days.

Which one should a beginner pick?

  • If you want the simplest first step: Robinhood — lower qualifying bar, longer claim window, and a stated cash-withdrawal timeline.
  • If you were already planning to fund $100+ and open a Webull brokerage account anyway: Webull's referral is a reasonable add-on, but judge it on the platform, not on the typical ~$3 in shares.
  • If you want both: that is allowed in principle — they are separate platforms — but do not deposit money you cannot afford to leave invested just to chase a small fractional-share reward. Investing involves risk, including losing money.

Neither offer is guaranteed returns or easy money. Both pay in shares/stock, whose value can fall as well as rise.
