I Have $100 to Start Investing: Robinhood or Webull?

Short answer: $100 is exactly the amount that matters for Webull — it is the deposit that triggers Webull's referral. For Robinhood, $100 is just a starting deposit; Robinhood has no $100 bar for its referral, and its referrer reward runs on milestones instead. Which one fits depends on whether you want the lowest bar or the larger welcome bundle.

The $100 rule, plainly

Webull: the friend must open a standard brokerage account and deposit $100 within 30 days. That $100 deposit is what triggers the referral. Prediction-markets-only signups do NOT trigger it. The referrer typically gets fractional shares worth about $3, and the friend's welcome offer is 10 fractional shares (about 98% at $3 each, range $3–$300) on a $100+ deposit, plus 1 month of Premium — check live terms, because welcome offers change. Full details: Webull: The $100 Deposit Rule and Webull Referral Explained.

Robinhood: the friend must be new, approved, link a bank/funding source, and make a qualifying deposit — there is no $100 deposit bar stated for the referral. The friend usually gets a stock worth about $5 (about 99% of the time; range $5–$200). The referrer's reward is milestones only: $50 at 1 funded referral, $150 at 3, $800 at 10, $4,000 at 50, $10,000 at 200, with a $15,000 annual cap. Full details: Robinhood Referral Explained.

What $100 actually buys

On either platform, $100 is your money to invest — the referral bonus is separate and small. With fractional shares on both platforms, $100 can buy a slice of a stock or ETF that costs more than $100 a share; you do not need $100 to buy one whole expensive share. The $100 does not become the bonus, and the bonus does not replace the $100. Investing involves risk: the $100 you deposit can lose value, and the free stock or fractional shares can also lose value. Only invest money you can afford to lose.

Who each suits

  • Pick Robinhood if you want the lowest deposit bar, the clearest cash-out timeline (sellable after 3 trading days, cash withdrawable after 30 days), and you are referring friends at volume — the milestone ladder only pays meaningfully at 3+ referrals.
  • Pick Webull if you already have $100 ready to deposit within 30 days, you want the larger welcome bundle (10 fractional shares + Premium month), and you are opening a standard brokerage account — not prediction markets only.
  • Pick neither yet if the $100 is money you need in the next month. The 30-day withdrawable-cash timeline on Robinhood and the deposit commitment on Webull both assume the money can stay invested.
