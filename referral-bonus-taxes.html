Are Referral Bonuses Taxable? 1099 Rules for 2026

Short answer: yes. A referral bonus is taxable income — cash, free stock, gift cards, credits, and bonus entries all count at fair market value. The 2026 change is about paperwork, not taxability: the 1099 reporting threshold rose from $600 to $2,000, so most readers will not receive a form — and still owe tax on what they received.

The 2026 rule change, plainly

The old $600 threshold everyone quotes is wrong for 2026 payments. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act (Section 70433, P.L. 119-21) raised the general 1099 reporting threshold from $600 to $2,000 for payments made after December 31, 2025, and the IRS confirmed the $2,000 figure in Publication 1099 and in the Instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC (Rev. December 2026). The threshold is aggregate per recipient per calendar year: ten separate $200 rewards to the same person total $2,000 and cross the line. Starting in 2027 the $2,000 figure adjusts annually for inflation.

The threshold governs the payer's paperwork, not whether you owe tax. A $500 bonus is still $500 of taxable income with no form. No form arriving does not mean no tax is owed.

The general rule is that referral fees are taxable ordinary income (26 USC 61, 26 CFR 1.61-1), and non-cash rewards are taxed at fair market value.

1099-MISC vs 1099-NEC vs W-2

  • Customers (you, referring friends): most customer referral rewards go on Form 1099-MISC, Box 3 (Other income).
  • Contractors / affiliates in an ongoing arrangement: payments for services in an ongoing promotional arrangement go on Form 1099-NEC, Box 1.
  • Employees: an employee referral bonus is supplemental wages reported on Form W-2. The aggregate withholding method is the default; the 22% flat rate applies only if the bonus is paid or identified separately.

Some thresholds did not move with the 2026 change: royalties still trigger a form at $10, and gross proceeds paid to an attorney still trigger at $600. Those exceptions do not apply to ordinary customer referral bonuses.

Self-employment tax — only if it's a business

A casual referrer owes no self-employment tax. Self-employment tax of 15.3% applies only if referring is a trade or business with $400 or more in net earnings. Do not assume a casual reader who referred a few friends owes self-employment tax — they almost certainly do not.

What to keep

  • A screenshot of the bonus value at receipt (the stock price, the credit amount, the entries credited) with the date.
  • The platform's tax documents, if any arrive.
  • Dates of each referral and each payout.
  • If no form arrives, the amounts are still reportable — your records are the only proof of what you received.

FAQ

Are referral bonuses taxable?

Yes. Cash, free stock, gift cards, credits, and bonus entries are all taxable income at fair market value, under the general rule that referral fees are taxable ordinary income (26 USC 61).

What is the 1099 reporting threshold for referral bonuses in 2026?

$2,000 per recipient per calendar year, for payments made in 2026. It was $600 for payments made in 2025. The threshold is aggregate per recipient, not per payment.

Do I owe tax on a referral bonus if I never got a 1099?

Yes. The threshold only decides whether the payer must send you a form. A bonus below $2,000 is still taxable income, and no form arriving does not make it tax-free.

Is free stock from Robinhood or Webull taxable?

Yes, at its value when you receive it — and that value becomes your cost basis, so a later sale is taxed only on the gain above it.

Are Underdog bonus entries / Polymarket trading credits taxable?

Entries and credits are not cash, so the bonus itself is not money in your pocket. Winnings from them are taxable. No published IRS rule settles exactly when a non-withdrawable credit or entry becomes taxable, so this page states the known facts and labels the timing as unsettled rather than guessing.

What form do referral bonuses go on — 1099-MISC or 1099-NEC?

Most customer referral rewards go on 1099-MISC, Box 3 (Other income). Payments to contractors or affiliates in an ongoing arrangement go on 1099-NEC, Box 1.

Do I owe self-employment tax on referral bonuses?

Only if referring is a trade or business with $400 or more in net earnings — then 15.3%. A casual referrer owes no self-employment tax.

Are employee referral bonuses taxed differently from customer referral bonuses?

Yes. An employee referral bonus is supplemental wages on Form W-2, with aggregate withholding as the default (22% flat only if the bonus is paid or identified separately). A customer referral bonus is reported on 1099-MISC Box 3, if a form is required at all.

Is the $600 threshold still the rule?

No, not for 2026 payments. The $600 threshold applied to payments made in 2025, reported in early 2026. For payments made in 2026, the threshold is $2,000. Any page still stating $600 as the current rule was written before the change took effect — including some pages updated only in May 2026.

What records should I keep for referral bonuses?

Screenshots of the bonus value at receipt with dates, the platform's tax documents if any arrive, and the dates of each referral and payout. If no form arrives, your own records are what you report from.
