The $25 credit, step by step

  1. The friend signs up with the referral.
  2. The friend deposits $10.
  3. Both the referrer and the friend receive $25 in trading credit each.
  4. The credit is credited within 7 days of all requirements being met.

That last point is the 7-day clock in the title: it is the crediting window — how long it can take for the credit to arrive after you qualify — not a claim that the credit expires in 7 days. Do not confuse the two.

What the credit is not

  • It is not $25 in cash. It is trading credit.
  • It is not directly withdrawable. You trade with it; the credit itself does not convert to withdrawable cash on arrival.
  • It is not unlimited. The referrer cap is 50 referrals, which works out to $1,250 in credit at $25 per referral.

On expiry/use windows for the credit itself: secondary sources differ, so this page does not assert a single expiry length. Check Polymarket's live incentive terms for the current use window before relying on the credit for a specific trade.

Do not confuse this with the fee-share program

There is a separate, international fee-share program that is sometimes written about alongside this offer. It is not this offer. Key distinction, as checked in October 2026:

  • This page — US refer-a-friend: $25 trading credit each after a $10 deposit, up to 50 referrals. No $10,000 trading-volume threshold is part of this offer as described here.
  • International fee-share program: a share of referred users' trading fees, which needs $10,000 in lifetime trading volume first. That is a different program with different rules.

If a guide promises fee-share earnings from a US refer-a-friend signup, it is mixing up two programs. Use the $25-credit description above for the US referral link on this page.

Who this suits — honestly

This suits someone who already wants to trade on prediction markets and would deposit $10 anyway. It does not suit someone looking for $25 in withdrawable cash, because that is not what the credit is. Prediction-market trading can lose money; never deposit money you cannot afford to lose just to chase a credit.
