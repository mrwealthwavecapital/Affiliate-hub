Robinhood Referral Bonus Not Received? What to Check First

Your friend signed up through your Robinhood referral link and nothing showed up. Before concluding the bonus is lost, work through these seven checks in order — most missing Robinhood bonuses are a missed qualifying step, a clock that has not run out yet, or a reward sitting in a different account from the one you are looking at. All figures below are from Robinhood's referral terms as checked in October 2026.

Disclosure: I earn a commission if you sign up through my link. Terms were checked in October 2026 and terms change — check Robinhood's live referral terms and the rewards section of its app before concluding a bonus is lost.

Check 1 — Did the friend actually qualify?

The referral counts only if the friend is a new customer who is approved, links a bank, and makes a settled deposit. An existing account, a rejected application, or a deposit that never settled all mean no referral — and no bonus for either of you.

Check 2 — Are you looking in the right account?

The friend's free stock (usually $5, range $5–$200) is claimed in the friend's account; the referrer's milestone lands in the referrer's rewards. Check the right account before anything else.

Check 3 — Did the 60-day claim window pass?

The friend must claim the free stock within 60 days of it being offered or it lapses. A stock never claimed is gone, not delayed.

Check 4 — Are you expecting a per-referral payment that no longer exists?

Since September 2026, Robinhood pays referrers on milestones only: $50 at 1 funded referral, $150 at 3, $800 at 10, $4,000 at 50, $10,000 at 200, with a $15,000 per-year cap. One referral earns $50; the second earns nothing until the third unlocks $150. Older sources citing a $1,500 cap are superseded by the milestone structure, and the research does not say whether milestones stack — check your own rewards section.

Check 5 — The stock arrived but the cash did not

That is normal, not a missing bonus. The stock is sellable after 3 trading days, but cash is withdrawable only 30 days after the deposit. Use the cash-out calculator to get the exact dates for your deposit.

Check 6 — Are you still inside the posting window?

Referral rewards are not instant. If the friend's deposit settled only a few days ago, the reward may simply not have posted yet. Wait out the window the app shows before escalating.

Check 7 — What to do if all seven checks pass and there is still nothing

Open Robinhood's help in the app, go to the rewards section, and check the referral status line for that friend — it usually names the missing step. If the status shows qualified and the reward is still absent after the window, that is the point to contact support with the friend's signup date and deposit date.
