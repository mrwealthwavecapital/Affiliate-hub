How Long Does a Webull Referral Take? The Full Timeline

A Webull referral has three clocks running at once, and they are easy to confuse: a deposit window, a posting window, and a claim window. This page lays out the timeline in order, so you can see which deadline applies when — and which dates this page cannot give you, because the research does not state them.

The timeline

  1. Day 0 — sign up through the referral link and open a standard brokerage account. The referral only triggers on a standard brokerage account. Prediction-markets-only signups do not trigger it, and joint, IRA, and entity accounts are excluded.
  2. Within 30 days — deposit $100 or more. The friend must deposit at least $100 within 30 days of signing up. Miss this window and the referral does not trigger.
  3. About 10 days after the deposit — the reward posts. The reward typically posts within about 10 days of the deposit. "About" is doing real work here: this is a typical posting time from the October 2026 summary, not a guaranteed date.
  4. Within 30 days of the reward being issued — claim it in the app. The reward must be claimed in-app within 30 days of issue. This is a second 30-day window, separate from the deposit window, and missing it can mean missing the reward.

Put together, the realistic path is: sign up, deposit $100+ within a month, see the reward post roughly ten days later, and claim it promptly. From signup to a claimed reward, expect roughly five to six weeks if everything goes on schedule — and do not plan around the earliest possible date.

The two 30-day windows

The deposit window and the claim window are the two places this referral fails most often, because they look alike and are counted from different starting points.

  • Deposit window: 30 days from signup. The deposit must be $100 or more, into a standard brokerage account.
  • Claim window: 30 days from the reward being issued. The reward is claimed in the app, and the clock starts when Webull issues it, not when you signed up.

If you sign up and deposit on day 29, you are inside the deposit window — but the reward will not post until around day 39, and the claim clock then runs from that issue date. The safe habit is to claim the reward the week it posts, not the week the claim window ends.

What each side gets

The friend's welcome offer is 10 fractional shares, worth $3 to $300 each, with about 98% at $3 — plus one month of Premium. The referrer typically receives fractional shares worth about $3, with a range of $3 to $300 depending on the campaign. Both sides should expect the small end of the range: most rewards are about $3, and the larger amounts are the exception, not the norm.

What this page does not say — and why

The October 2026 research does not state Webull's sell or withdrawal timing for referral shares. This page therefore gives no sell date and no withdrawal date for Webull. Do not assume Robinhood's windows apply here — Robinhood's 3-trading-day sellable date and 30-day withdrawal date are Robinhood's terms, not Webull's, and this page will not present them as Webull's. If you need to know when referral shares can be sold or when cash can be withdrawn, check Webull's live terms before you sign up.

What does not trigger the referral

  • Prediction-markets-only signups. Opening only a prediction-markets account does not trigger the referral. The standard brokerage account and the $100+ deposit are what matter.
  • Joint, IRA, or entity accounts. These are excluded from the referral.
  • A deposit under $100, or a deposit after the 30-day window. Either one can mean no reward.
