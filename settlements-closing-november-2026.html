Five rules before you file anything

  1. Only file if you truly fit. Claims are sworn statements. Guessing your way into a class is not a harmless lottery ticket.
  2. Filing is free on official sites. Type the official address directly. Do not click links in emails about settlements.
  3. Never pay a finder. Anyone charging you to file a free claim is taking money for nothing.
  4. Keep your proof before you start. Receipts, notices, and screenshots are much harder to find after you have begun.
  5. Deadlines are filing dates, not payout dates. Class actions typically pay 6–18 months after filing. File on day one because the window closes; do not count the money until it arrives.
