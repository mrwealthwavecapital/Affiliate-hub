How Many Referrals Does It Take to Make $100?

Four referral programs, four very different answers — and only one of them ends in $100 you can actually withdraw. The rest pay in shares, bonus entries, or trading credit. Here is the math, done honestly, from figures checked in October 2026.

Robinhood: 3 referrals — the only one that ends in cash

Since September 2026, Robinhood pays referrers on milestones only: $50 at 1 funded referral, $150 at 3, $800 at 10, $4,000 at 50, $10,000 at 200, with a $15,000/year cap. There is no $100 milestone — 3 referrals reaches the $150 one.

One caveat: the research does not say whether milestones add up, so this page treats each milestone as the amount at that count and does not add them together. Older sources cite a $1,500/year cap; the milestone structure is newer — check live terms.

Each referred friend must be new, approved, link a bank, and make a qualifying deposit. The friend usually gets a $5 stock (range $5–$200). The referrer's milestone is paid in stock too: sellable after 3 trading days, with cash withdrawable 30 days after the deposit, and the stock must be claimed within 60 days. Robinhood is the only program on this page with a stated path from referral reward to withdrawable cash.

Polymarket US: 4 referrals — fastest to $100, but it is credit, not cash

Polymarket US refer-a-friend pays $25 in trading credit per referral, to the referrer and the friend, after the friend deposits $10 — up to 50 referrals ($1,250 total). Four qualifying referrals is $100 of credit, which makes it the fastest route to $100 of value on this page.

The catch is what the $100 is. Trading credit is not cash and can never be withdrawn as cash directly — it must be traded first, and only proceeds above the credit from those trades may be withdrawable, under the live terms. The credit lands within about 7 days; its expiry length differs across sources and is not verified here. Two conflicts to flag: one source reports a $350 cap instead of $1,250, and this refer-a-friend program is entirely separate from Polymarket's fee-share program, which needs $10,000 of lifetime trading volume before it pays anything.

Underdog: 10 referrals — $100 of entries, never cash

Underdog pays the referrer $10 in bonus entries per referred friend, credited within about 72 hours of the friend's $10 deposit. Ten friends is $100 in bonus entries. Some guides report $10–$25 by campaign; $10 is the consistent baseline. Each referral also carries a 1-in-1,000 shot at a $2,000 jackpot — about $2 of expected value each, but any single referral is almost certainly $10.

Bonus entries are entries, not money. They can be used to enter fantasy contests, they expire 14 days after being credited, and they can never be withdrawn as cash. Only winnings from contests entered with them can become cash. So Underdog reaches $100 of value in 10 referrals, but $100 of value that can never leave the app as cash.

Webull: about 34 referrals — the slowest road

Webull pays the referrer fractional shares per successful referral, typically worth about $3 — about 98% of awarded shares are about $3, with a range of $3–$300. At $3 each, $100 takes about 34 qualifying referrals (100 ÷ 3 = 33.3, rounded up). Some sources describe the reward as "up to 20 free stocks"; that figure conflicts with the ~$3 research and is flagged here, not used for the math.

The bar is also the highest on this page: each friend must open a standard brokerage account and deposit $100+ within 30 days. Prediction-markets-only signups do not trigger the referral, and joint, IRA, and entity accounts are excluded. The shares post within about 10 days and must be claimed in-app within 30 days of issue. The October 2026 research did not state a sell or withdraw timeline for Webull rewards, so this page does not guess at one — do not assume Robinhood's 3-day / 30-day windows apply.

The honest ranking

If the goal is $100 you can withdraw, Robinhood wins at 3 referrals — it is the only program here with a stated cash-out path. If the goal is $100 of value fastest, Polymarket wins at 4 referrals, but the value is trading credit that must be traded before any of it can become cash. Underdog's 10 referrals buy $100 of contest entries that expire in 14 days. Webull's ~34 referrals buy about $100 of shares behind a $100-per-friend deposit requirement.

Behind all four numbers: a referral only counts when the friend finishes the whole qualifying step. A signup that never deposits earns nothing anywhere — count only friends who would actually deposit.
