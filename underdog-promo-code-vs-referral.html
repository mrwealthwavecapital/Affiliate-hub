Underdog Promo Code vs Referral Link in California: What's the Difference

Search for an Underdog offer and you will find two different things with similar names: referral links and promo codes. They are not the same deal, they do not pay the same people, and they do not come from the same place. This page explains the difference, and what each one means if you are in California.

Two different things with similar names

A referral link belongs to an ordinary account holder. It is the in-app refer-a-friend program: one person shares their link, a friend signs up through it, and the referrer gets a reward. There is no separate public revshare "affiliate program" for ordinary users — refer-a-friend is the program that exists for individuals.

A promo code shown on a review site or in media is usually a different, partnership-based deal between Underdog and a publisher. Those codes are not the same as a friend's referral link, and the terms attached to them can be different — different amounts, different qualifying actions, different expiry. A code you find on a review site was negotiated by that publisher; a referral link was shared by a person.

What the referral link does

When a friend signs up through a refer-a-friend link and deposits $10, the referrer gets $10 in bonus entries. Several guides add that the friend must also play or enter once. The credit lands within about 72 hours. The reward is bonus entries for fantasy contest entries — not cash, and not withdrawable. Unused bonus entries expire in 14 days. There is one bonus per friend. There is also a 1-in-1,000 chance the bonus is $2,000 instead of $10.

The friend enters the referrer's username as the referral code at signup. The friend's own side is the welcome offer, which is separate from the referral reward.

What a promo code usually offers

The welcome offer attached to promo codes is commonly "Play $5, Get $50" in bonus entries — deposit $10, enter $5. Some partner pages show a 50% first-deposit match up to $1,000 instead. Welcome offers rotate, so never quote one without checking the live offer in the app. The point is not the exact current amount: it is that a promo code's offer is a welcome bonus for the new player, set by a partnership deal, while a referral link's reward is a bonus for the person who shared it.

Do not assume a promo code and a referral link can be combined, or that one is a better version of the other. They are separate mechanisms. If you have both in front of you, read the terms of each in the app before choosing, and do not trust a review site's summary over the live offer.

What you can actually play in California

This is the part that matters most for a California reader, and it applies to both promo codes and referral links. Underdog does not operate a traditional sportsbook in California — online sports betting is not legal in the state. Never take a promo code or referral link as a way into a California sportsbook, because there is not one.

What is live in California is Underdog DFS — drafts, Best Ball, and Champions contests — and Underdog Predict, its prediction-market product. Classic Pick'em in California is contested: two current guides list Pick'em in only 15 states that do not include California, while another state table lists California as available. A 2025 California Attorney General opinion concluded pick'em and draft-style DFS contests constitute illegal sports wagering; the Governor's office disagreed, and no enforcement has followed. This page does not claim Pick'em is available in California either way — verify it inside the app before entering.

Which one should you use?

  • If a friend shared a referral link with you: you are using the refer-a-friend program. Your friend gets $10 in bonus entries when you deposit $10, and you get the welcome offer in effect at signup.
  • If you found a promo code on a review site: you are using a publisher partnership offer. Read the live terms in the app — the amount, the deposit requirement, and the expiry are set by that deal and can differ from what the review site says.
  • If you are choosing between them: compare the live welcome offer in the app against the other, and remember that the referrer's $10 in bonus entries is a separate reward from the friend's welcome offer. The question is which welcome offer you get, not which label sounds bigger.
